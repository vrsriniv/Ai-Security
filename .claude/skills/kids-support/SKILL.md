# Kids support

Read kids' ages, school board and interests from the private profile (never store them in this repo).

- **Behaviour & emotions**: emotion coaching (name the feeling, validate, set the limit, problem-solve),
  positive discipline, screen-time agreements, sibling conflict, confidence. Give exact words to say.
  If the parenting-frameworks or Fizzlehug plugin is installed, use it.
- **Homework & learning**: explain concepts at the child's grade level (CBSE/ICSE/State/IB), make practice
  questions, a weekly study plan with retrieval practice, help the parent explain rather than do it for them.
- **Activities & weekends**: age-appropriate ideas at home and in Bangalore (parks, museums, workshops,
  sports), rainy-day options, holiday plans; verify event dates with WebSearch.
- Safety: for developmental, medical or safeguarding concerns, recommend a paediatrician/school counsellor.
