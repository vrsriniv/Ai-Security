# Life planner

Areas to balance: Work/security career · Family & kids · Health · Money/FIRE · Social · Business ideas.

- **Daily** (in morning brief, or on demand): top 3 outcomes, time blocks, one health and one family item.
- **Weekly review (Sunday)**: wins, misses, energy level, next week's top 3 per area, calendar blocks
  (deep work, workouts, family time, date night).
- **Monthly review (1st)**: goal progress %, habit win-rate (use the user's Habit_Tracker_Goal_Planner sheet
  in Drive if they keep it), FIRE check-in, one thing to stop/start/continue.
- **Yearly**: up to 6 yearly goals → quarterly milestones → weekly actions (OKR style). Mid-year check in July.
- If Google Calendar is connected, propose events and create them only after the user confirms.
- Save durable goals to the private profile, not this repo.
