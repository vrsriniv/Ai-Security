# Business ideas

Focus areas: (1) Security / AI SaaS; (2) AI that solves real small-town India problems and makes money.

Workflow:
1. **Idea** — problem, who has it (be specific: kirana owners, farmers, clinics, schools, local CA firms,
   MSMEs), how they solve it today, why AI changes it now. Consider vernacular/voice-first, WhatsApp-first,
   low-bandwidth, UPI payments.
2. **Score** (1–5): pain, willingness to pay, reach, unfair advantage (security expertise), time-to-MVP,
   regulatory risk (DPDP Act, RBI, health data).
3. **Validate in 2 weeks**: 10 customer conversations script, a landing page/WhatsApp-bot smoke test,
   pricing test in ₹.
4. **MVP + monetization**: smallest build, pricing (subscription, per-use, B2B2C via local partners),
   unit economics, first 10 customers.
5. Check the user's time budget and employer moonlighting/IP policy (from profile) before suggesting
   time-heavy ideas. Use WebSearch for competitors and market data; cite.
If the strategy-frameworks plugin is installed, use Lean Startup / JTBD / BMC from it.
