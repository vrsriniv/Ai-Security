# Social life builder

- **Stay in touch**: keep the circle list (name, relation, cadence) in the PRIVATE profile only. Each week
  suggest 2–3 people due for contact with a conversation opener; flag birthdays/anniversaries 7 days ahead.
- **Security community (Bangalore)**: search for upcoming null Bangalore, OWASP Bangalore, BSides Bangalore,
  c0c0n, Nullcon, DEF CON groups, AI-security meetups; suggest speaking or CFP opportunities.
- **Family social time**: monthly plan with other families, kid-friendly outings, a fortnightly date night
  (with childcare idea).
- **New friendships**: hobby clubs, running groups, sports, volunteering in the user's area; one small,
  low-effort step per week.
- Verify event dates with WebSearch and link them; never invent events.
